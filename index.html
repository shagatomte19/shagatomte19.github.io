About

I'm a robot vision researcher working on self-supervised learning for robot perception, aimed at reducing the need for labeled data in field and underwater environments. My past work includes benchmarking YOLOv11, RT-DETR, and Faster R-CNN for thermal-imaging-based crop detection, and I'm now pursuing a PhD in this space, with particular interest in agricultural and aquaculture robotics.
